Five-line summary
The 86.8% estimate suggests most MYPE lacked a RUC in 2024, but it is from ComexPerú—not an official PRODUCE census.
The 69.3% figure describes workers in informal jobs over July 2025–June 2026; it must not be used as the share of unregistered businesses.
PRODUCE counted about 2.33 million formal micro and small firms in 2024 under its administrative activity criteria.
SUNAT’s regime totals indicate substantial taxpayer populations, but they do not establish how many MYPE belong to each regime; the General-Regime and MYPE-only split remain unknown.
A large potential accountant audience exists, but current firm counts and the share of small businesses outsourcing accounting were not verified; validate willingness to pay and client mix directly with accountants.
