# Aldía: closest competitors and adjacent tools

## Findings

The closest adjacent products are accounting/tax-control platforms (CumpliTax, NubeCont, ContaUno, iGEA ERP and iSiore GO) and electronic-invoicing/PSE/OSE providers (especially Efact). Broader ERP and sales platforms such as Siigo Nube Perú and Bsale overlap on SUNAT-linked records but are not documented as specialized credit-tax validators. NubeFact, Digiflow, EDICOM and Sovos/Paperless focus more on document issuance, reception, technical validation, integrations and archiving than bookkeeping for accounting firms.

**Five closest to Aldía:**
1. **CumpliTax** — closest positioning: automatic XML/content, SUNAT status and RUC checks, plus a claimed 140+ rules and evidence workflow. These claims lack independent product testing.
2. **NubeCont** — accounting-firm panel, mass XML/CDR validation and retrieval, Excel imports, and SIRE comparison. Full statutory creditability rules are not verified.
3. **ContaUno** — accountant multi-company workflow, SUNAT validity and issuer checks, SIRE/PLE and document extraction; user/accountant review remains part of the workflow.
4. **iGEA ERP** — separate accounting by RUC, SIRE/PLE, bank matching and automated basic document checks, but no verified complete credit-tax rule engine.
5. **Efact** — supplier-receipt and automatic SUNAT validation claims, positioned to reduce credit-tax risk; no proof of full accounting-level eligibility adjudication.

**Does anyone automatically validate crédito fiscal?** Public evidence does not independently establish a product that automatically assesses every substantive SUNAT creditable-VAT condition. CumpliTax makes the strongest broad claim; Efact claims automatic SUNAT/CPE validation and credit-fiscal risk reduction. These should be treated as vendor claims, not independently demonstrated performance. SUNAT status, XML/UBL validation, CDR acceptance, or SIRE/PLE processing is not by itself proof of credit-tax eligibility. Human per-invoice review is often not stated; iSiore explicitly leaves final acceptance to a responsible reviewer.

**Potential gaps in the documented market:** publicly verifiable rule coverage and accuracy; transparent human exception/review controls; invoice OCR; consistent bulk incoming-invoice entry and XML retrieval; clearly defined multi-RUC workflows and per-RUC pricing; and clear boundaries between SIRE/PLE preparation and tax-return filing.

This comparison covers 12 new companies and excludes those already named in the brief. Unknown means not verified in reviewed public sources, not necessarily absent.
